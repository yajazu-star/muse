Treatments

What we treat

Each treatment begins with a consultation and a hand-drawn design. Most clients complete their result over two to three sessions, spaced about two weeks apart. Pricing is confirmed at consultation, based on the area and coverage required.

01

Hairline Restoration Receding hairline · M-shaped loss

We rebuild a frontal hairline that suits your face, age and future hair loss pattern — soft and age-appropriate, never a hard stamped edge. The design is drawn, adjusted and approved by you before a single impression is placed.

02

Hair Density Thinning crown · Diffuse thinning

For hair that is thinning but still present, pigment is layered between existing follicles to reduce the contrast between hair and scalp. The result reads as visibly thicker, fuller hair — particularly effective for crown and parting lines.

03

Female Hair Loss Widening parting · Postpartum · Diffuse loss

A discreet density treatment designed for women's patterns of loss, assessed on the Ludwig scale. Impressions are placed along the parting and crown to restore the appearance of coverage without shaving the hair.

04

Scar Camouflage FUE · FUT strip scars · Injury scars

Transplant and injury scars are blended into the surrounding scalp by replicating follicle impressions across the scar tissue, matched to its texture and tone. Scar tissue is assessed first, as it holds pigment differently from healthy skin.

05

Alopecia Alopecia areata · Totalis · Universalis

Full-scalp and patch coverage for alopecia clients, planned carefully around active and stable areas. For totalis and universalis, we design a complete, natural-looking shaved-head effect including hairline and crown swirl.

06

Beard Enhancement Patchy beard · Beard density

The same follicle-replication technique applied to the beard: filling patches, sharpening cheek and jaw lines, and increasing the visual density of facial hair for a groomed, even finish.

SMP Correction Blown-out dots · Blue-grey fade · Poor hairlines

Work done elsewhere can often be rescued. We assess oversized or migrated impressions, colour shift and unnatural hairlines, then advise honestly whether correction, lightening referral, or a combination will restore a natural result.

Expertise

The science behind a natural result

SMP looks simple — thousands of tiny dots. Done properly, it is applied skin science: pigment chemistry, dermal depth control and optical design working together. This is the knowledge every SCALPIST SMP treatment is built on.

The Korean 5D method

A real shaved follicle is not a uniform dot. It varies in size, sits at a slight angle, and is surrounded by thousands of others at irregular intervals. The 5D method controls five variables of every impression so the eye reads pigment as hair, not ink:

DiameterDot size matched to your natural follicle calibre
DepthConsistent placement in the upper dermis
DirectionAngle following your natural growth pattern
DensityLayered spacing built up over sessions
DimensionTonal gradation — darker at crown, softer at hairline

Pigment & skin science

SMP pigment is placed into the upper dermis, around 0.8–1 mm deep — shallower than a body tattoo. That depth is the whole craft: too deep and impressions migrate and blur (“blow-out”); too shallow and they shed with the epidermis within weeks.

We use carbon-based pigments formulated for the scalp, diluted to a grey-scale matched to your skin undertone and hair colour. Unlike tattoo inks containing blue and green colourants, correctly selected SMP pigment fades neutrally over the years instead of shifting blue — the most common failure we correct from work done elsewhere.

  • Assessment scales — Norwood (men) and Ludwig (women) grading at consultation
  • Undertone matching — pigment tested against your skin before session one
  • Healing cycle — sessions spaced ~2 weeks so each layer settles before the next

Hygiene & safety protocol

SMP breaks the skin, so it is held to a clinical standard in our studio — the same discipline expected of any procedure involving needles and bloodborne-pathogen risk.

  • Single-use sterile needles — opened in front of you, disposed of in a sharps container
  • Barrier protection — machine, cord and surfaces wrapped and replaced per client
  • Medical-grade disinfection of all contact surfaces between appointments
  • Screening first — we defer treatment for active scalp conditions, keloid tendency or broken skin, and will tell you plainly if SMP is not right for you

Longevity & aftercare

A completed result typically holds its definition for four to six years before a light touch-up session refreshes it. Longevity depends on sun exposure, skin type and immune response — oily skin and heavy UV exposure soften impressions faster.

Aftercare is simple but not optional: keep the scalp dry and sweat-free for the first days, avoid sun, swimming and saunas while the micro-scabs shed (about a week), and use SPF on the scalp thereafter. We give you a written aftercare plan and check in on your healing.

  • Day 1–4 — no washing, no sweating, no touching
  • Day 5–7 — gentle cleansing; flaking is normal
  • Week 2+ — next session once fully settled

How to judge SMP quality

Look at healed work, not fresh work — fresh impressions always look darker and crisper than the settled result. Healed SMP should show soft, matte, slightly varied dots with no shine, no uniform “helmet” edge, and a hairline that breaks up microscopically at the front. If every dot is identical, the eye detects the pattern. Variation is what makes it invisible.

The process

From consultation to completion

Step 1

Consultation & design

We assess your hair loss pattern, skin type and goals, then draw your proposed hairline or density map directly on the scalp. You approve the design — and can live with it under the studio lights and in photos — before anything permanent happens. About 30 minutes.

Step 2

Session one — the foundation

The first layer of impressions is placed at conservative density and tone. This sets the hairline and overall coverage. The scalp looks slightly darker immediately; mild redness settles within 24–48 hours. Typically 2–4 hours depending on area.

Step 3

Session two — density & depth

About two weeks later, once the first layer has healed and lightened to its true tone, we build density between the original impressions and deepen the tone where needed. This is where the three-dimensional, textured effect appears.

Step 4

Session three — refinement

A final detailing session: softening the hairline edge, balancing tone across zones, and perfecting transitions at the temples and crown. Some clients are complete in two sessions; the third is included in the plan whenever the result calls for it.

Questions

Before you book

Does SMP hurt?+

Most clients describe it as mild scratching rather than pain — the scalp is less sensitive than most tattoo areas, and impressions are placed very shallowly. Discomfort varies by zone (the temples and hairline are more sensitive) and most clients complete sessions without any anaesthetic.

How many sessions will I need?+

Two to three sessions for most treatments, spaced roughly two weeks apart. Building pigment in layers is what keeps impressions crisp: a single heavy session risks blow-out and uneven fading. Your plan is confirmed at consultation.

Will it look natural if my hair loss continues?+

Yes — this is planned for at design stage. Hairlines are drawn conservatively for your age and projected Norwood pattern, and density work can be extended in future sessions as thinning progresses. SMP also combines well with a later hair transplant.

Do I need to shave my head?+

Only for hairline and full-coverage work, where SMP replicates a shaved look. Density treatments for thinning hair and female hair loss are performed through your existing hair with no shaving required.

How long does it last?+

Typically four to six years before a single touch-up session restores full definition. It fades gradually and evenly rather than disappearing in patches.

Can SMP fix a bad result from another studio?+

Often, yes. Correction is one of our specialities — we assess dot size, depth, colour shift and hairline shape, then advise whether corrective layering will restore a natural look or whether laser lightening should come first. Book an SMP Correction consultation and bring photos if you have them.
